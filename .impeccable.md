## Design Context

### Users
Prospective investors and operating partners in the oil & gas / energy sector. These are sophisticated institutional professionals — fund managers, O&G executives, government-linked entities — who assess credibility in seconds and are allergic to hype. They arrive skeptical, need to feel trust immediately, and will only make contact if the site signals competence, stability, and opportunity without ever asking for it directly. Secondary users include due-diligence teams who need governance docs and detailed project data.

### Brand Personality
**Sophisticated, Warm, Grounded.** AEI is a seasoned advisor, not a startup pitch. The voice is confident without being boastful, precise without being cold. There's a human warmth beneath the professionalism — these are people who put their own money alongside their investors'. The brand conveys: *"We've been here before, we know this landscape, and we'd be good partners."*

### Emotional Goals
The primary emotion is **confidence and trust** — "These people know what they're doing. My money is safe here." Every design decision should reinforce perceived stability, expertise, and institutional-grade seriousness. The opportunity (Indonesian energy) should feel inevitable and well-understood, not speculative.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Two modes, one identity:**

1. **Landing page & marketing pages** (Home, About, Team, Contact): Editorial, cinematic authority. Inspired by [sonauraenergy.com](https://sonauraenergy.com) — clean section hierarchy, strong visual storytelling, frosted glass overlays on video/imagery, measured scroll pacing. Indonesian ocean and deep-water imagery grounds the brand in place. Generous whitespace, restrained color use, type-driven hierarchy through weight and scale.

2. **Block detail pages** (Gaea, Gaea II, Talu, etc.): Bloomberg-style data density. Accolades, projected production numbers, partner logos, interactive charts (Plotly), timelines, news feeds. Information-rich, scannable, confidence-building through sheer depth of data. Still within the same token system and visual identity, but the layout shifts from editorial to dashboard-adjacent.

**Reference sites:**
- [sonauraenergy.com](https://sonauraenergy.com) — primary reference for landing/marketing pages
- Bloomberg / The Economist — reference for block detail page data density and editorial authority

**Anti-references (explicitly avoid):**
- Crypto / startup landing pages: no neon gradients, particle effects, hype language, countdown timers
- Generic corporate templates: no stock-photo grids, blue-and-grey sameness, cookie-cutter SaaS layouts
- Flashy creative agencies: no wild type experiments, chaotic layouts, style-over-substance

### Design System Summary
- **Colors:** Cool blue-grey neutral scale (#151E25 dark through #F5F9FC light), deep teal primary (#12587c), cyan alternate (#0aadd6), muted cyan accent (#62caeb). The palette is intentionally cool and oceanic — evoking deep water, calm authority, and vast Indonesian seascapes. Warm accent colors (amber, coral) may be reintroduced later as complements; any additions must harmonize with the established blue-teal foundation. Both dark and light themes supported.
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans — a single geometric sans-serif designed by an Indonesian foundry, chosen for thematic resonance and practical simplicity. All hierarchy is achieved through weight (400–700) and size, not font-family contrast. Three token aliases exist (`font-sans`, `font-serif`, `font-body`) but all resolve to Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Glass:** Frosted glass cards (`.glass-card`, `.glass-card-dark`) are a signature pattern. Both themes define glass tokens with calibrated opacity, blur, and shadow. Use over video/imagery for text readability. Don't overuse on plain backgrounds.
- **Motion:** GSAP ScrollTrigger for scroll-driven entrances. Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Animations should feel measured and purposeful — no bouncing, no playfulness. Ease curves should suggest weight and precision (power3.out, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)).
- **Imagery:** Indonesian landscapes — ocean, deep water, volcanic ridges, tropical light. Conveys frontier, scale, natural resource wealth. Video backgrounds on hero sections only.

### Design Principles

1. **Trust before flash.** Every visual choice must reinforce credibility. If a design element doesn't build confidence, remove it. Quiet authority beats spectacle.

2. **Earned density.** The landing page earns attention through editorial pacing — one idea per section, generous space, deliberate reveals. Block detail pages earn trust through data density — charts, numbers, timelines, partner logos packed tight. Match density to context.

3. **Grounded in place.** Indonesia is not abstract — it's deep ocean, volcanic ridges, tropical light. The color palette (ocean blues, deep teal, cool grey-blues) and imagery should always connect back to the physical landscape where AEI operates. The typeface itself (Plus Jakarta Sans) has Indonesian roots.

4. **Warmth through craft.** The site should feel handmade, not templated. Custom glass effects, considered weight/scale typography, scroll animations that respond to the user — these details signal care and competence. But never ornament for its own sake.

5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Never rely on color alone. All interactions keyboard-navigable. Reduced-motion fallbacks for every animation. Investor audiences include older professionals — legibility and clarity are non-negotiable.
